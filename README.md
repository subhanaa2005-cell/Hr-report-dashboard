# Hr-report-dashboard
📊 HR Analytics Dashboard
Overview
This project presents an interactive HR Analytics Report designed to analyze employee attrition trends. The dashboard provides insights into workforce demographics, job roles, education fields, and salary distribution, helping HR professionals identify patterns and make data-driven decisions.

Key Metrics
<br>
Total Employees: 1,000

Attrition: 237

Average Salary: 6.50K

Attrition Rate: 16.12%

Features<br>
Attrition by Education Field: Breakdown across Life Sciences, Medical, Marketing, Technical Degree, HR, and Others.

Attrition by Job Role: Highlights roles with highest attrition (e.g., Laboratory Technician, Sales Executive, Research Scientist).

Attrition by Age & Gender: Comparative analysis of male vs. female attrition across age categories.

Attrition by Working Years: Line chart showing attrition peaks at early (1 year) and mid-career (10 years).

Cross-tab Analysis: Attrition counts by job role and education field.

Visualizations<br>
📈 Line Chart: Attrition vs. total employees by working years

📊 Bar Charts: Attrition by job role, age category, and gender

🥧 Pie Chart: Attrition distribution by education field

📋 Table: Attrition counts across job roles and education fields

Purpose<br>
This dashboard enables HR teams to:

Identify high-risk job roles and education fields

Understand attrition trends by age and gender

Support strategic workforce planning

Improve retention strategies with data-driven insights
